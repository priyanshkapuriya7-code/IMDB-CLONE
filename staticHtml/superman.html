About

Superman is a revolutionary superhero film that reimagines the classic DC character for a modern audience. Director James Gunn brings a fresh perspective to the Man of Steel, focusing on the internal conflict between Clark Kent's dual identity and his responsibility to Earth.

The film features spectacular action sequences that showcase Superman's incredible powers while exploring deeper themes of identity, belonging, and what it truly means to be a hero. With outstanding performances and stunning cinematography, this Superman film stands apart from previous adaptations.
