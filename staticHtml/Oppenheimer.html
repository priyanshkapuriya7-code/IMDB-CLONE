About

Oppenheimer is a biographical film directed by Christopher Nolan that chronicles the life of J. Robert Oppenheimer, the theoretical physicist who led the development of the atomic bomb. The film explores his journey from brilliant scientist to military leader, and the profound moral conflicts he faced as his creation became a symbol of mass destruction and nuclear power.

Released in 2023, the film received critical acclaim and multiple award nominations for its compelling narrative, outstanding performances, and Nolan's masterful direction. It delves deep into the politics of World War II, the competition between nations to develop nuclear weapons, and Oppenheimer's personal struggle with his legacy.
